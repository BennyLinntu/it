01

Tokenize

The sentence is lowercased and split into words, keeping contractions such as “don't” intact.

02

Score

A curated emotion lexicon assigns weights, while intensifiers, diminishers and negations adjust them.

03

Normalize

Positive weights become a five-way distribution. The highest share is the dominant emotion.
